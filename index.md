I am a PhD candidate at the **London School of Economics** working on **income dynamics and inequality** and how redistributive policies can have very different current and lifetime impacts on low-income workers.

I also work on the dynamics of **urban planning** development — whether urban planning is warranted or should be left to market forces and, if so, how to best design it.

**I am on the 2026/27 economics job market.**

My job market paper is [*Does the Minimum Wage Reduce Inequality? Current vs Lifetime Perspectives*](/research/).

I hold an MRes and MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics, and a BSc in Economics from Nova SBE.

Here you'll find more about my [research](/research/) and [teaching](/teaching/), or you can download my [CV](/files/CV_FranciscoLibanoMonteiro.pdf).
